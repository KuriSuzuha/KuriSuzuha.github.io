01 / ABOUT

About

My personality type is probably INFJ, and I enjoy learning and sports.

Starting in my sophomore year, I gradually felt that theoretical work wasn’t my strength, and doing experiments required equipment and resources. So I became interested in Vibe Coding, because it makes it easy for me to turn my ideas into reality.

Besides this hands-on practice, I often conduct thought experiments. I’ve been interested in popular science books since childhood, especially the history of modern physics, cosmology, and theories of time; they nourish my thinking. Inspired by Steins;Gate, I’ve been trying to use philosophical reasoning to construct a “Convergence Theory” as a methodology of my own. It’s still a work in progress.
